About

Finance, Treated as What It Actually Is: A Probability Problem

Most investing content sells certainty. We think that's the wrong product. Markets are stochastic. Good decisions are made under uncertainty, not despite it — and the right math makes that uncertainty usable instead of terrifying.

Why This Site Exists

There's a gap in personal finance content. On one side: generic advice — "diversify," "think long-term," "don't time the market" — true, but stated with no mechanism, no math, no way to check the claim yourself. On the other side: institutional quantitative research, locked behind jargon, paywalls, and degrees most readers don't have.

The Stochastic Edge sits in between. Every article shows its work: the formula, the Python implementation, the assumptions, and — critically — the uncertainty. When we say "P50 outcome," we mean it literally: a median of thousands of simulated paths, not a vibe.

How We Operate

  • Every model is open. Python code ships with every quantitative article. Run it yourself, change the assumptions, see what breaks.
  • Every claim is checkable. We link primary sources and cite the actual data behind a figure rather than repeating secondhand summaries.
  • Affiliate relationships never dictate conclusions. We disclose every commercial relationship in full on our Affiliate Disclosure page, and a tool review is written the same way whether or not it monetizes.
  • Nothing here is personalized financial advice. See our full Disclaimer. We teach frameworks; you apply them to your own situation, ideally alongside a licensed advisor for anything high-stakes.

What You Won't Find Here

No price predictions. No "this stock is going to 10x." No urgency-manufactured pitches. Probability distributions don't traffic in certainty, and neither do we. If an article can't show its assumptions and its uncertainty, we don't publish it.
