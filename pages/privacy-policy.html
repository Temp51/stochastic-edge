1. Information We Collect

When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. When you browse the site, our analytics provider (e.g. Google Analytics or Plausible) may collect anonymized usage data: pages viewed, time on site, general location (country/city level), and device/browser type.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used solely to send the newsletter and toolkit you requested, and occasional relevant updates. We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address to third parties. Analytics data is used in aggregate to understand which content is useful and improve the site.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to operate this site, including an email delivery provider (e.g. Beehiiv or ConvertKit), a web analytics provider, and affiliate tracking links (see our Affiliate Disclosure). Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing the data they process on our behalf.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to remember your theme preference (light/dark mode) and, if you accept, to power anonymous analytics. You can decline non-essential cookies via the banner shown on your first visit, or block them entirely in your browser settings.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, unsubscribe via the link in any email or contact us directly — see our Contact page.

6. Data Retention

We retain your email address for as long as you remain subscribed. Upon unsubscribing, your address is removed from active sending lists within 30 days, subject to our email provider's own retention practices.

7. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to us via the Contact page.
